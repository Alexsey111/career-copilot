# Review Lifecycle

Generated content is draft material until a human reviews it.

Typical lifecycle:

1. Generate document or interview prep output.
2. Attach provenance and confidence.
3. Aggregate blockers, warnings, and recommended actions.
4. Show the review summary in the UI.
5. Human confirms or edits unsupported claims.
6. Approve and activate only after the review step.

The review layer should preserve explainability and avoid hiding unresolved claims behind a polished rendering.
